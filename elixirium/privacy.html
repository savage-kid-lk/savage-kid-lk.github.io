Elixirium Privacy Policy

Elixirium: Orb Sort Alchemy (Android)

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version
  • Elixirium has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your progress, lives, stars, Aether Dust and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, using Google AdMob. Google collects certain device and usage data to deliver and measure those ads.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states), we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and the game is not directed at children under 13.

2. Information we process

2.1 Game data stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves on your device: your level progress and stars, Aether Dust balance, lives and their refill timer, the cosmetics you own and have equipped, an unfinished level so you can resume it, tutorials you have seen, and your settings (sound, haptics, symbols). This data never leaves your device to us. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your identity.

2.2 Android backup (Google)

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the game data above in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google Account and encrypts. It can then be restored when you reinstall Elixirium or move to a new phone. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to your backups. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

2.4 Google Play

Downloads, updates, reviews and any purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. If we offer an in-app purchase (such as "Remove Ads"), Google processes the payment. We receive only confirmation of the purchase, never your payment details.

2.5 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and use it only to reply and keep a record of the request.

We do not collect your name, contact list, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera data, or any special personal information (such as health, religion or biometrics).

4. Advertising and your choices

  • Opt-in ads only. Ads appear only when you tap a button to watch one, for example to get an extra life when you are out of lives, use a booster for free, or earn Aether Dust. You never have to watch an ad to play.
  • Consent form. On first launch, if you are in a region that requires it, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Change your mind any time: open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. The button appears whenever your region requires one.
  • Advertising ID: on Android go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device) to reset or delete your advertising ID. When it is deleted, apps cannot use it for personalisation.
  • Google ad personalisation: manage it at My Ad Center.
  • Ad content: we limit ads in the game to content Google rates as suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (maximum rating "PG").

5. Who we share it with

We do not have servers that receive your personal information, so we do not pass it to anyone ourselves. Information is processed by:

  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited: AdMob advertising, the consent platform, Google Play and Android backup. Google may work with certified ad partners to fill ad requests, as described in Google's policies and in the consent form where applicable.
  • Authorities, if we are legally required to disclose information.

No sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA), letting Google use identifiers for personalised advertising may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising, or as targeted advertising. You can opt out at any time through Settings → Ad privacy choices or by deleting your advertising ID. Opting out does not affect how the game works.

6. International transfers

Google may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where personal information is transferred out of the EEA, UK, Switzerland or South Africa, Google relies on recognised safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and equivalent UK and Swiss mechanisms. This also meets the cross-border transfer conditions of section 72 of POPIA. See Google's Ads Data Processing Terms.

7. How long it is kept

  • On-device game data: kept until you reset progress in the game, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Android backups are kept according to your Google Account settings.
  • Advertising data held by Google: kept according to Google's retention policies (see How Google retains data).
  • Emails to us: kept for as long as needed to deal with your request, and then for up to 24 months unless the law requires longer.

8. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it. Communication between the ads SDK and Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because we hold no personal data on our own systems, the risk of a breach on our side is minimal. If a security compromise affecting your personal information ever occurs, we will notify you and the relevant regulator where the law requires it.

9. Children

Elixirium is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country (up to 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app tells Google not to treat ad requests as directed at children, and limits ad content to a "PG" rating. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it. You can also reset your child's advertising ID, or uninstall the app.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the rights below. Because we do not store personal information on our own systems, many requests are fully met by the on-device controls in section 11, but you can always contact us.

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR / Swiss FADP)

You have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing (including objecting to processing based on legitimate interests), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

South Africa (POPIA)

You have the right to know whether we hold your personal information and to access it, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to object to its processing (including for direct marketing), and to complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

United States (California and other state privacy laws)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, used and disclosed, to access, correct and delete it, to opt out of its sale, of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising, and of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. You may use an authorised agent, and we may need to verify your request.

How to exercise your rights

Email kekanaletago58@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will respond within one month (GDPR and POPIA) or 45 days (US state laws), and will tell you if we need more time as the law allows. For data that Google holds, we will direct you to Google's tools, which are the fastest way to act on it.

11. Deleting your data

  • Game progress: in the game open Settings → Reset level progress, or clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Elixirium → Storage → Clear storage), or uninstall the app.
  • Backups: manage or delete device backups in your Google Account (Android Settings → Google → Backup).
  • Advertising data: delete or reset your advertising ID, change your choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices, or use My Ad Center.
  • Emails: ask us to delete our correspondence with you.

Elixirium has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete.
